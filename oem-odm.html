MOQ, sample and lead time

Order terms depend on specification and packaging

OEM orders are not one fixed price. MOQ and delivery time depend on tape size, density, color, packaging material and artwork confirmation. Trial orders can be discussed when the packaging is simple or when we can use an existing SKU.

MOQDepends on product specification, spool color, tape color and packaging complexity. Standard SKUs usually support lower trial quantities than fully customized packaging.
SampleFactory-standard samples can be sent for tape testing. OEM samples are prepared after artwork and packaging details are confirmed.
ArtworkAI, PDF, EPS or high-resolution image files are preferred for logo, label, barcode and carton design.
Lead timeStandard packing is faster. OEM packaging usually needs extra time for artwork proof, sample confirmation and packing material preparation.
