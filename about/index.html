PTFE tape factory for practical B2B supply

How we work

Clear communication before production

A clear inquiry helps both sides avoid wrong specifications, wrong packing and slow quotation. We usually confirm the order in a simple sequence.

1. Product targetConfirm product type, width, length, thickness, density or target roll weight.
2. Packing targetConfirm bulk packing, shrink wrap, bag, blister card, display box, private label or export carton.
3. Sample checkUse sample, photo or packaging artwork to confirm the style before bulk order.
4. ProductionArrange slitting, winding, packing and internal checking according to confirmed details.
5. Shipment supportPrepare carton details, packing photos and basic export packing information.
