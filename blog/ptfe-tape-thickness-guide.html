Thickness, density and net tape weight

Thickness describes the film dimension. Density describes mass per unit volume. Net tape weight is the tape material on one roll, excluding the spool, cover and packaging. Record these as separate fields.

Thickness · mm

The nominal film thickness and agreed tolerance.

Density · g/cm³

A separate material-control value; thicker does not automatically mean denser.

Net tape weight · g

The tape material per roll. Whole-roll weight also includes the spool and cover.

For example, our 0.10 mm options include different densities. Two rolls with the same thickness, width and length are not necessarily the same product. Compare all specification fields before comparing price.

How to choose a thickness

  1. Start with the required product or an existing sample. Record its thickness, width and actual length. For a replacement product, use the fitting or project requirements rather than guessing from spool colour.
  2. Compare like-for-like rolls. Align width, length, density or net tape weight, spool and packing. A thicker roll with a different density or shorter length is a different quotation.
  3. Confirm the sample and acceptance basis. Agree on thickness tolerance, inspection method and packing before ordering in bulk.

PTFE tape thickness FAQ

How thick is PTFE tape, often called Teflon tape?

There is no single thickness for all PTFE thread seal tape. Our current catalogue includes nominal 0.075 mm, 0.10 mm and 0.20 mm options. These equal 75, 100 and 200 microns. Check the exact product specification rather than assuming every roll is the same.

Is a mil the same as a millimetre?

No. One mil is one thousandth of an inch, equal to 0.0254 mm or 25.4 microns. For example, 0.10 mm is approximately 3.94 mils, not 0.10 mil.

Is thicker PTFE tape always better?

No. Thickness is one dimension, not a quality grade. Match the intended product specification, width, length, density or net tape weight, fitting requirements and approved sample. A thicker tape is not automatically a higher-density tape.

What is the difference between 0.075 mm and 0.10 mm tape?

The nominal thickness differs by 0.025 mm, or 25 microns. A useful buying comparison also holds width, roll length, density or net tape weight and packing on the same basis. Thickness alone does not tell you which finished roll contains more material.

Can I request 0.15 mm or another thickness?

Send the requested thickness, width, length, quantity and any sample reference for confirmation. The chart lists selected current catalogue options; it does not establish availability, tolerance or lead time for every custom thickness.

How should thickness be checked before a bulk order?

Agree on the nominal thickness, tolerance, measurement method and sampling plan before production. Record readings with an appropriate calibrated gauge under the agreed conditions. Keep net tape weight separate from the weight of the spool, cover and packaging.
