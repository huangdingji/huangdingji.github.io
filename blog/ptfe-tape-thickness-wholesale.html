Compare the two thickness references on the same product and packing basis—then approve the finished roll, not just the number printed on a label.

Compare 0.075mm and 0.1mm on the Same Basis

The thickness value describes one part of the finished tape. It does not by itself identify material amount, roll length, winding, packing or suitability for a particular connection. The useful question is not simply “Which thickness is better?” but “Which complete SKU matches the buyer's approved reference and sales channel?”

Avoid a false comparison: a 0.1mm quotation and a 0.075mm quotation are not directly comparable when length, density, net tape weight or packing are different.

Thickness, Density and Net Tape Weight Are Different

Thickness is a dimensional field. Density describes mass per unit volume. Net tape weight is the tape material on one finished spool, excluding the spool and packing. A supplier quotation should keep these fields separate instead of using one as a vague substitute for another.

Thickness

Record the nominal target and the agreed tolerance or sample reference.

Density

Record a target only when the buyer needs it as a specification or control field.

Net tape weight

Use an agreed weighing basis when material amount per roll matters.

Finished sample

Use the accepted roll to control appearance, winding, spool and packing together.

For the broader relationship between all product fields, see the PTFE tape specification checklist.

Approve the Finished Roll Before a Bulk Order

  1. Assign a sample reference and record its target specification.
  2. Check the dimensions and agreed material-control fields.
  3. Review winding, tape surface, spool condition and unwind behavior.
  4. Confirm label, individual pack, inner pack and export carton presentation.
  5. Keep the accepted sample or signed specification as the repeat-order reference.

Hand feel can help a buyer compare samples, but it should not replace recorded specification fields. When a measurement matters to acceptance, agree on the value, tolerance and inspection basis before production.

Control Thickness Claims on OEM Labels

Do not finalize private-label artwork while the tape specification is still changing. The thickness printed on a label, card or box should match the approved order specification and agreed tolerance.

  • Freeze the finished roll specification before final artwork approval.
  • Keep technical values separate from marketing names and spool colors.
  • Confirm barcode, label size, language and carton mark with the buyer.
  • Do not add pressure, temperature, gas, potable-water or compliance claims without buyer-approved evidence and destination review.

For customized labels and cartons, review the OEM PTFE tape packaging process.

Thickness Does Not Approve the Application

A thicker roll is not automatic proof of suitability for gas, potable water, pressure, temperature or any regulated use. Approval depends on the finished product, fitting and thread, media, operating conditions, installation method and applicable project or destination requirements.

The supplier can quote and produce against a defined finished-roll specification. The buyer, importer, installer or responsible project party should confirm application suitability and required evidence.

Send a Quotation-Ready Thickness Brief

A useful first message can be one line per SKU:

Example structure: target width × stated length × thickness; density or net tape weight if required; tape/spool colors; individual packing; carton quantity; order quantity; destination; sample reference.

If one of these fields is unknown, send the existing product photo or sample and mark the missing value for factory confirmation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 0.1mm PTFE tape always better than 0.075mm tape?

No. Thickness alone does not establish quality or application suitability. Compare width, stated length, density or net tape weight, tolerance, winding, packing and the approved sample.

Are thickness and density the same specification?

No. Thickness is a dimensional field, density describes mass per unit volume, and net tape weight records the tape material on one finished roll. Record them separately when they matter to the purchase.

How should wholesalers compare two PTFE tape quotations?

Ask both suppliers to quote the same width, stated length, thickness target, density or net tape weight, spool, individual packing, carton quantity and quantity tier. Resolve missing fields before comparing unit prices.

Can PTFE tape thickness be printed on an OEM label?

It can be printed when the value and tolerance agree with the approved order specification. Do not finalize artwork until the finished tape and inspection basis have been confirmed.

What information should a buyer send for a thickness quotation?

Send the target width, stated length, thickness, density or net tape weight if known, tape and spool colors, packing, quantity, destination and any sample reference.
