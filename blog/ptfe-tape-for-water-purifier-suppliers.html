Tape reference · pack fit · kit quantity

PTFE tape for water purifier kits: specify the roll and the pack.

Compare existing roll references, check the finished unit pack and prepare a clear quantity plan for each purifier kit.

Direct answer

Specify the tape, the finished pack and the kit quantity separately. Use the purifier model and parts list to define the accessory. Confirm the intended connection, document requirements and actual pack fit before approving a kit sample.

Start from the purifier model and its parts list

Record the purifier model, kit revision and the role of the tape: an item in an installation bag, a separately sold accessory or a service-team supply. Identify the tape as its own part, with a supplier option and a defined number of rolls per kit.

Check the actual connection before including tape. Push-fit tube connections can use an O-ring seal, while a separate threaded end has its own requirements. Use the connector manufacturer's instructions for each end. The connection brief shows what information to collect.

Treat special short rolls as a separate request

If the kit brief calls for a 1m, 2m or 3m roll, send the complete requirement for feasibility review. Confirm width, thickness, density or net tape weight, spool or buckle format, finished dimensions and sample arrangements. Those lengths are not presented here as confirmed catalogue SKUs.

A shorter tape length does not prove that the finished roll fits a smaller space. The spool and cover can determine its outside dimensions. Confirm the actual finished pack before changing the kit layout or comparing the unit cost.

Approve the tape and the packed kit together

  1. Product check: confirm the tape option, actual dimensions, agreed weight basis, winding and finished spool or cover.
  2. Packing check: put the finished unit pack in the actual kit with the other parts. Check dimensions, count, closure and identification against the kit revision.
  3. Document review: identify the connection use and the exact documents your team requires. Record open points independently of the physical packing check.
  4. Repeat-order reference: keep the approved sample, model, parts-list revision, tape option and artwork together. Agree on substitutions before production.

Confirm artwork and drinking-water document scope

Share the destination market, intended connection use and the exact document or listing your customer requires. Match the product identity, manufacturer, scope and applicable conditions before approving any related label wording. A document for another fitting or purifier is not evidence for this tape option.

The catalogue data above provides no drinking-water certification claim. Certification of a component and certification of the finished system are separate matters. Confirm the required evidence for the selected product and its intended role.

For OEM bags or cards, provide the kit/model reference, product text, rolls per unit, language, barcode artwork and revision. Request a physical packing sample; printing, construction, MOQ and lead time are confirmed for that design. See the OEM process for artwork and packing coordination.

Purifier-supplier buyer questions

Does every water purifier connection need PTFE tape?

No. Identify the specific connector and follow its manufacturer's instructions. A push-fit tube connection can seal with an O-ring; tape is not automatically needed because the fitting belongs to a purifier. Review any separate threaded end on its own basis.

Can I order a 1m, 2m or 3m short roll?

Treat those lengths as a custom request until the complete specification, spool format, sample, MOQ and availability are confirmed. This guide compares existing 7m and 10m catalogue references and does not list a confirmed 1m, 2m or 3m SKU.

Is the 7m roll necessarily smaller than the 10m roll?

Tape length alone does not define finished-roll size. Confirm outside diameter and overall height with the selected spool and cover, then check the finished unit pack against the kit space.

Do these catalogue options establish drinking-water certification?

No. They provide dimensional and weight references. State the document and intended-use requirements, then verify the exact tape product and scope before approving related wording. Product, kit-fit and document reviews are separate checks.

How does the kit quantity calculator work?

It multiplies your entered number of kits by rolls per kit, then adds the additional spare rolls you enter. It counts physical rolls for planning; it does not estimate how much tape a purifier installation needs or confirm MOQ.

Which sample should I approve before kit production?

Review the selected tape, finished spool and cover, completed unit pack, artwork and its fit in the actual kit layout. Record the purifier model and kit parts-list revision so repeat orders use the same reference.
