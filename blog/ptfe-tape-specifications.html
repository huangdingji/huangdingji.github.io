PTFE Tape Specifications Explained

Build a quotation that can be compared line by line: dimensions, density or net tape weight, color, spool, individual packing and export carton details.

B2B buyer guide

This guide explains the main PTFE tape specification fields buyers should confirm before quotation: width, length, thickness, density, color, spool and packaging method.

In this guide Buyer checkpoints Product guidance Wholesale sourcing notes Related reading FAQ

Buyer checkpoints

  • Confirm width, length, thickness and density before comparing prices.
  • Check tape color, spool color and packaging method for the target sales channel.
  • For OEM orders, confirm label, barcode, carton mark and artwork requirements early.
  • Send packing method and any known size or quantity details when requesting quotation.

A usable specification describes the finished roll and its finished pack. If one supplier quotes only dimensions while another includes density, individual wrap and a printed box, their unit prices are not directly comparable.

The fields every quotation should show

WidthState the tape width in millimetres and confirm how it will be measured.
LengthState the nominal roll length and request the same basis from every supplier.
ThicknessState the film thickness and the agreed tolerance or sample reference when required.
Density / net weightUse density and/or net tape weight to distinguish rolls that share the same outer dimensions.
Color and spoolSpecify tape color, spool color, spool format and any printed or molded detail.
Individual packingDefine loose, wrapped, labeled, boxed or carded presentation.
Carton packingConfirm units per inner pack, units per export carton, carton dimensions and carton mark.
DocumentsList only the reports or declarations actually required by the buyer or destination.

Thickness, density and net tape weight are different

Thickness is a dimensional measurement. Density describes mass per unit volume. Net tape weight is the tape material on one spool, excluding the spool and packaging. These fields are related, but one should not be used as a substitute for the others without an agreed calculation and measurement method.

For sample comparison, record all three when they matter to the buyer. A “high density” label without a value, roll-weight target or approved sample is too vague for repeat procurement.

Color does not define the technical grade

Tape and spool colors help buyers organize product lines and retail presentation. They do not independently prove pressure, temperature, media compatibility or regulatory approval. For gas-related or other regulated channels, confirm the finished specification, evidence and label wording required for the destination.

Buyer questions

Which specification should a new buyer start with?

Start with the product already accepted in the target market, or send a physical sample and sales-channel description. The factory can then identify the missing fields for a comparable quote.

Why ask for net tape weight?

It helps distinguish material amount when rolls have similar dimensions. The measurement method and tolerance should be agreed before it becomes an order-control field.

How do I convert a quote to price per metre?

Divide the finished-roll price by the stated length, then compare packing and material amount separately. Price per metre alone does not align density, net tape weight or packaging.

How should gas-related tape be specified?

Begin with the buyer-approved application requirement, then confirm dimensions, density or weight, color, documents and label wording. Color alone is not approval.

Quotation-ready summary

Send the factory one line per SKU with the product specification, packing, quantity and destination requirement. Mark unknown fields clearly rather than guessing; they can be closed during sample confirmation.
