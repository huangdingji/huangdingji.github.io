What the Official BIS Record Says

The Bureau of Indian Standards lists a product specification specifically for this category. The record should be read precisely: the existence of an Indian Standard is not the same as a current Quality Control Order making certification compulsory.

Official titleUnsintered polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) tape for thread sealing applications — Specification

Time-sensitive information: this status was checked on 7 October 2026. QCOs, standards and customs requirements can change. Recheck the official portals before each regulated order or shipment.

Voluntary Certification Is Not the Same as a QCO

BIS product certification is generally voluntary unless the Central Government makes compliance compulsory for a product through a Quality Control Order or another applicable measure. The current BIS record for IS 14643:1999 says “Voluntary Certification.”

That classification does not prevent a buyer, tender, project owner, marketplace or regulated sales channel from requesting IS 14643 compliance, an ISI-marked product, test evidence or specific label wording. It also should not be treated as a permanent exemption from future QCO changes.

  • Check the current BIS record for IS 14643.
  • Search the latest BIS compulsory-certification and QCO pages.
  • Confirm tender, project and end-customer requirements separately.
  • If a supplier claims a BIS licence or ISI mark, verify the licence through official BIS channels.

Checks to Complete Before Ordering

The Indian importer should establish the compliance and customs route before approving packaging or shipping documents. The factory can then prepare the quotation and sample against the buyer-confirmed requirement.

1. Define the product

Confirm whether the item is unsintered PTFE thread seal tape and record the finished specification.

2. Check the channel

Identify wholesale, retail, tender, project or other destination-specific requirements.

3. Verify the rule

Check BIS status, current QCOs and buyer-required standards before artwork approval.

4. Confirm customs

Importer or broker fixes classification, duties and required documents before invoicing.

A supplier should not promise that one document package will clear every transaction. The importer of record and its customs or compliance adviser should confirm the requirement for the exact product, origin, sales channel and shipment date.

Customs Classification, Import Policy and Duties

This page does not assign one universal Indian tariff code or duty rate to every PTFE tape shipment. Classification may depend on the exact product description, construction, presentation and the customs interpretation applied to the transaction.

Before the commercial invoice is finalized, the Indian importer or customs broker should confirm the Customs Tariff Head or ITC (HS) code, import policy, effective duties, country-of-origin treatment and any compulsory compliance requirement. ICEGATE provides an official Trade Guide on Imports for searches by tariff heading or description.

Avoid fixed landed-cost claims: duty, tax, surcharge, preferential treatment and local charges can change. Use the importer or customs broker's current calculation for the actual shipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an Indian Standard for PTFE thread seal tape?

Yes. BIS lists IS 14643:1999, “Unsintered polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) tape for thread sealing applications — Specification.” The BIS record shows the standard was reviewed in 2025.

Is BIS certification compulsory for PTFE thread seal tape in India?

The BIS standard record reviewed on 7 October 2026 classifies certification for IS 14643:1999 as voluntary. Because QCOs and import requirements can change, the Indian importer should recheck the current BIS compulsory-certification pages and transaction requirements before shipment.

Does voluntary certification mean no checks are needed?

No. A buyer, tender, project or regulated sales channel may require a particular standard, test report, licence or label even where BIS certification is not generally compulsory. Confirm the requirement for the actual transaction.

Which HS code and import duty apply to PTFE tape in India?

This page does not assign a universal tariff code or duty rate. The Indian importer or customs broker should classify the exact product and verify current duties, import policy and compulsory compliance requirements through ICEGATE before the invoice is finalized.

What should an Indian buyer send for quotation?

Send the target tape dimensions, density or net tape weight if known, tape and spool colors, packing method, quantity, destination, sample reference and any buyer-required standard or document.
