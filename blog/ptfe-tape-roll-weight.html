PTFE tape roll weight: net vs gross.

There is no single weight for every PTFE tape roll. Net tape weight covers the film only; the complete roll also includes the spool and any other stated components.

Direct answer

Compare the same roll specification and the same weighing basis. Our Option 12 and Option 13 both measure nominally 12mm × 10m × 0.1mm, but their catalogue net tape references are 4.2g and 5.4g. Density is different; a complete roll on a scale is a different weight basis.

State what the weight includes

“Roll weight” is ambiguous unless the included components are identified. Keep the following fields separate when comparing supplier quotations.

Weight fieldIncluded components
Net tape weightPTFE film only, excluding spool, cover, label and individual packing.
Finished-roll weightTape and spool, plus the cover or label when included. Record the exact assembly.
Packed-roll weightFinished roll and its stated bag, shrink wrap, card or box.
Carton gross weightRolls, inner packs, outer carton and all other included packing materials.

Tare is the non-tape mass removed from a total when determining tape-only mass. Record which components were measured and subtracted.

Align the roll before comparing grams

Keep width, actual length and thickness the same, then compare density or net tape weight. Also identify the spool, cover and packing whenever the quotation uses a complete-roll or packed-roll figure.

A wider or longer roll can contain more material without having a higher density. For example, the 19mm × 50m high-density option is a different roll from the 12mm × 10m examples above; comparing their gram values alone does not isolate density.

Use the specification checklist and thickness guide to keep the dimensional fields consistent.

Use a repeatable weighing method

  1. Identify the sample. Record the option or sample ID, lot reference and agreed dimensions.
  2. Define the weight field. Specify net tape, finished roll or packed roll, and list included components.
  3. Check the instrument. Use a suitable calibrated scale, confirm its unit and resolution, and zero the agreed weighing setup.
  4. Measure the sample and matched tare. If deriving net tape mass, record the non-tape components included in the same total. Use the tare method agreed with the buyer.
  5. Record the result. Keep individual readings, date, scale reference, target and agreed tolerance. Apply the accepted sampling and decision rule.
Net tape mass = measured total mass − measured non-tape mass included in that total.

The tare must match the components and method. An assumed empty-spool weight does not establish the actual tape-only result.

A blank record for your sample checks

Use one record per inspected sample. The template is blank; it contains no claimed factory readings, pass results or acceptance limits.

A heavier roll needs an explanation

Extra weight may be tape, spool, cover or packaging. Even the same net tape weight does not establish identical dimensions, winding or surface condition. Check weight alongside the other agreed product requirements.

Keep carton weight on its own basis

Carton gross weight includes the final rolls and all packing materials. Confirm rolls per inner pack, inner packs per carton, carton dimensions and included components. See the carton packing guide for the fields to prepare.

Keep the repeat-order reference consistent

Save the approved sample, specification, weighing basis and tare method together. When a spool, cover or label changes, review whether the previous complete-roll weight reference still applies.

Roll weight questions

What is net tape weight?

Net tape weight is the mass of the PTFE film only. It excludes the spool, cover, label and individual packing. State the weight basis and agreed measurement method on the quotation.

Why are the 4.2g and 5.4g catalogue references different?

Options 12 and 13 have the same nominal 12mm width, 10m length and 0.1mm thickness, but density references of 0.35 and 0.45 g/cm³. Their listed net tape weights are 4.2g and 5.4g. These are catalogue references, not test results from the scale photograph.

Does the scale photograph prove net tape weight?

No. The photograph shows a roll assembly being weighed. It does not supply a matched tare record for the non-tape parts or identify the sample as Option 12 or 13. A net tape result needs its own defined measurement and tare basis.

How can net tape weight be determined from a complete roll?

Record the total measured mass and its included components, then subtract the measured mass of the non-tape components included in that same total. Agree on a matched tare method, sample selection and tolerance. Do not subtract an assumed empty-spool weight.

Is a heavier PTFE tape roll always better?

No. Extra weight can come from more tape, a different spool, a cover or packing. Compare the same width, length, thickness and weight basis, and review the remaining product requirements separately.

What should I send for a weight-matched quotation?

Send the product option or sample reference, width, actual length, thickness, density or target net tape weight if required, weighing basis, quantity, packing and destination. Mark unknown values for confirmation.
