PTFE tape for valve distributors: define the connection and the order.

Build a clear connection brief, compare actual tape specifications and link the approved sample to your accessory supply plan.

Direct answer

Start with the valve connection, then specify the tape. Confirm where the connection seals and which sealing method the valve or fitting manufacturer permits. Use a complete tape specification, an approved product sample and a separate application review to prepare the order.

Define the accessory you are buying

A valve distributor may buy tape as a separately stocked item, a contractor-counter supply or a proposed part of a valve kit. State which of these you need. A tape SKU in the same catalogue as a valve is a commercial association; a tape approved for a specific connection needs its own application reference.

Identify the connection

Valve item, end connection, sealing location and manufacturer instructions.

Define the tape sample

Option, dimensions, density or net tape weight and agreed tolerance.

Confirm the order record

Application review, packing, identification and supporting records.

Build the connection brief before matching a roll

Threads can fasten parts together while a different surface makes the seal. Record the actual end-connection designation and drawing reference, rather than inferring the sealing method from the valve name or appearance.

Connection informationWhat the buyer should confirm
Tapered pipe-thread connectionThe exact thread designation and the manufacturer's permitted sealant or tape for that connection and service.
Parallel thread with gasket or O-ringThe specified gasket, O-ring or sealing face. The presence of threads alone is not a reason to add tape.
Compression or ferrule connectionThe manufacturer's sealing arrangement and assembly specification; keep it distinct from a pipe-thread sealing requirement.
Connection not identifiedA valve part number, drawing or clear markings for review. Leave the application decision open until the connection is identified.

Add the mating-part material, intended medium, pressure and temperature requirements, and any documents your customer requires. The responsible application reviewer should use the actual manufacturer's instructions and service requirements. This procurement brief does not replace them.

Keep product checks and application review separate

Product sample record

Identify the tape option and sample. Record width, actual length, thickness, density or net tape weight basis, agreed tolerances, winding and spool or cover details.

Use the weight record method to distinguish tape-only weight from the finished roll.

Application review record

Identify the valve and mating connection, service conditions, manufacturer instructions, required evidence and the person responsible for the decision.

Record the review outcome and any open points separately from the tape sample approval.

For pre-dispatch evidence, agree which measurements, product and packing photos, identification fields and documents are required. Availability and scope are confirmed for the order; a product photo is not a connection test report.

On repeat deliveries, connect the purchase-order line, tape option, approved sample and shipment or lot identification where available. When reporting a discrepancy, provide measured differences and the measurement method so the same basis can be compared. See our quality review materials for original sample-check examples.

Choose separate resale or a defined kit plan

  • Separate tape cartons: give each tape option its own order line, roll quantity, inner pack and carton mark. Reference the related valve range on the buyer's item record.
  • Valve-kit proposal: define the exact kit item, tape option, rolls per kit and packing layout. Confirm the technical association and proposed kit sample before approving artwork.
  • Mixed accessory shipment: list valve and tape items separately on the packing plan. An assortment total does not replace quantities per tape SKU.

Pack and carton quantities depend on the chosen roll and format. Use the carton packing guide to confirm counts, outer dimensions and weight basis. No fixed carton count is assumed here.

Tie OEM wording to the approved item

Send the tape and valve or kit references with the proposed label, language, barcode, spool color and carton marks. Mark the artwork revision that belongs to the approved sample. Color and a shared brand alone do not establish application suitability.

If your customer requires an application claim or a specific document, state the exact requirement in the inquiry and confirm the evidence for the selected product. Keep draft wording separate from approved label text. Confirm MOQ, tooling or custom-material arrangements and lead time for the final specification and packing plan.

Prepare a sourcing record for each valve-accessory item

Download the blank record and keep one row per proposed tape-to-valve association. It joins the connection brief, tape option, sample and order identification without treating an unfinished review as approval.

Download blank sourcing record (CSV)

The file contains no customer case, test result or approval. Fill in the actual manufacturer references and review outcome for your order.

  • Connection: valve and mating-item references, end connection, sealing method and instruction revision.
  • Tape: option, dimensions, density or weight basis, tolerance and sample reference.
  • Review: service requirements, requested documents, reviewer, outcome and open points.
  • Supply: resale or kit plan, quantities per SKU, packing revision and shipment identification.

Valve-distributor buyer questions

Does every threaded valve connection need PTFE tape?

No. Identify the exact connection and where it seals. Some connections use a gasket, O-ring, ferrule or sealing face. Use the valve or fitting manufacturer's specified sealing method for that connection.

Can I choose tape from the nominal valve size alone?

Nominal valve size is not a tape specification. Include the end-connection designation and manufacturer reference, then confirm the allowed sealing material. The 19mm options here are catalogue comparisons, not a valve-size matching chart.

What is the difference between Options 32, 33 and 35?

All three are 19mm wide and 0.1mm thick. Options 32 and 33 are 20m long, with density 0.25 and 0.3 g/cm³ and net tape references 9.5g and 11.4g. Option 35 is 50m long at 0.3 g/cm³, with net tape reference 28.5g.

Does approving the tape sample approve the valve application?

No. A product sample establishes the agreed tape and packing reference. Application review uses the actual connection, service conditions, manufacturer instructions and required supporting documents. Record these as separate decisions.

Can tape be supplied with a valve accessory kit?

Share the valve or kit item reference, proposed rolls per kit, label text and packing layout. Confirm the tape specification and application review separately, then agree kit quantities, identification, sample approval, MOQ and lead time.

What should I send when a repeat delivery differs from the sample?

Send the order and tape option references, the approved sample reference, delivery or lot identifier if available, photos and measured differences with the method and weight basis. Keep a product discrepancy separate from a connection or application review.
