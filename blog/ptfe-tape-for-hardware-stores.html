Define the store item before choosing the range

For hardware-store supply, write down the item you want staff to receive, price and reorder: tape specification, spool and cover, rolls per selling unit and retail pack. If you already sell a reference product, send its stated dimensions and photos with a sample request.

Begin with your store's demand or an existing item to match. Add a wider roll or a different density only when it has a clear role in the assortment. The examples below help compare specifications; they do not predict which item will sell fastest in your market.

Approve a sample that staff can receive and sell

  1. Match the tape. Check width, length, thickness and the agreed density or net tape weight basis against the option reference. Use the net tape weight guide when comparing weight records.
  2. Check the finished roll. Confirm spool and cover fit, winding appearance and the approved color or marking. Keep a labeled sample reference.
  3. Check the selling unit. Count the rolls in the pack, read the specification and pack-count text, then check the finished label or pack at the counter.
  4. Check replenishment. Match inner packs and carton marks to the store SKU record. Record any approved change before accepting a repeat production run.

Make the label and barcode match the retail item

Confirm the brand, option reference, stated tape dimensions, rolls per selling unit, label language and artwork revision. Send the buyer-approved barcode artwork with the selling-unit definition.

Scan a finished packing sample in the store system and check that the displayed item and pack count match. A single roll, a retail multipack and a warehouse carton can be different item records; do not assume one barcode is suitable for every level.

If the barcode, pack count or tape specification changes, review the item record and artwork before reordering. See OEM/ODM options for the wider customization process.

State quantities per SKU, then confirm MOQ

Send the required roll quantity for each option and the rolls per retail selling unit. Keep those separate from the replenishment-pack and carton counts. For a mixed assortment, request a separate product and packing line for each SKU.

MOQ, sample arrangements, custom material requirements and lead time are confirmed for the selected product and packaging. The three references here are not a bundled stock offer or a fixed minimum-order programme.

Keep one approved record for each retail SKU

Use a record that links your store SKU to the supplier option, the sample and the packing revision. This gives the next buyer or warehouse colleague a reference beyond a photo or a short product name.

  • Product: option, dimensions, density or net tape weight basis and agreed tolerance.
  • Retail item: rolls per selling unit, spool/cover, label, barcode reference and artwork revision.
  • Replenishment: inner-pack count, carton plan and order quantity per SKU.
  • Approval: sample reference, review date, person checking and any agreed change.

Download blank assortment record (CSV)

One row per proposed or approved retail SKU. The file is blank: it does not contain an actual store order, sample approval or measured result. Keep barcode references as text in your spreadsheet.

Hardware-store buyer questions

Which PTFE tape should a hardware store stock first?

Start from the specification and selling unit your store needs. The three catalogue options above are sample-comparison references, not a sales ranking or a compulsory starter assortment. Use local demand and an approved sample to decide the range.

Are Options 12 and 13 the same because both are 12mm × 10m?

No. Both are 0.1mm thick, but Option 12 lists density 0.35 g/cm³ and net tape weight reference 4.2g; Option 13 lists 0.45 g/cm³ and 5.4g. Compare the full specification and agree on sample tolerances.

Is a shrink pack automatically a retail multipack?

No. Agree whether the pack is sold unopened as one item or opened for individual-roll sales. Record rolls per selling unit separately from rolls per replenishment pack and rolls per carton.

Can a yellow spool be used to identify gas tape?

Spool color is a presentation choice and does not establish the tape specification or application approval. Keep the supplier option and full specification on the SKU record; confirm any intended use separately.

Can I request private-label or hanging-card packing?

Send the proposed artwork and pack format with the selected tape option. Spool marking examples are shown here; card or display-box construction, barcode position, tooling, MOQ and lead time need a separate quotation and sample approval.

What should stay fixed when a store reorders?

Reference the approved option and sample, dimensions, density or net tape weight basis, selling-unit count, spool and cover, artwork revision, barcode reference, inner pack and carton plan. Agree on any substitution before production.
