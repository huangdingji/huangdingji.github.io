Roll · inner pack · export carton

PTFE tape carton packing: counts, weight and volume.

Confirm the rolls per inner pack and inner packs per carton for each SKU. Use the final outer dimensions, stated net-weight basis, gross weight and carton marks for the order.

Direct answer

Carton quantity is part of a specific packing plan. A tape size or roll weight alone does not determine how many rolls fit in an export carton. Start with the finished roll, confirm the inner pack, then approve the outer carton.

Confirm rolls per carton from the packing plan

For one SKU packed in equal-size inners, multiply the rolls in each inner by the confirmed number of inners in the carton. Count any loose rolls separately.

Rolls per carton = rolls per inner × inners per carton + loose rolls.

For the ten-roll shrink format shown above, use ten as the inner-pack count only when that format is approved for the selected SKU. Confirm the outer-carton arrangement with a packing sample.

Carton strength, packing protection, final gross weight and warehouse handling requirements also need review. There is no single preferred carton count for every spool or retail pack.

Keep SKU quantities easy to receive

Assign a packing reference to each carton configuration. For mixed-SKU cartons, list each SKU and its count, then reconcile the sum with the carton total and packing list. Confirm whether the buyer accepts mixed cartons before packing.

Keep product option, inner-pack quantity and carton marks consistent so receiving staff can identify the product without opening every carton.

Match carton marks to the approved record

Use the buyer-approved SKU names and quantities on both the carton and packing list. Agree on the required mark fields and artwork before printing.

  • Buyer or order reference and product/SKU description.
  • Roll quantity, with a per-SKU list for mixed cartons.
  • N.W. with its agreed net-weight basis; G.W. as packed.
  • Final outer dimensions and carton number, when required.

Calculate outer-carton volume (CBM)

For a rectangular carton: length × width × height in cm ÷ 1,000,000 = m³. Multiply by carton count only for cartons with the same outer dimensions.

Carton packing questions

How many PTFE tape rolls fit in one carton?

The quantity depends on the actual roll and spool size, inner packing, carton design and agreed handling requirements. Confirm a packing sample for the selected SKU. The photographs do not establish one universal rolls-per-carton count.

Does a 10-roll shrink pack determine the outer-carton quantity?

No. Ten rolls identifies the shrink-pack reference shown. The number of these packs in an outer carton must be confirmed separately for the selected product and carton.

How is carton volume in CBM calculated?

For a rectangular carton, volume in cubic metres equals outer length × outer width × outer height in centimetres divided by 1,000,000. Multiply by the number of cartons only when they have the same outer dimensions.

Is CBM the same as chargeable shipping weight?

No. CBM is a geometric volume. The carrier or forwarder applies the rules for the selected service, which may involve actual weight, volumetric weight and other handling conditions. Confirm freight on the final packing details.

What does carton net weight include?

Define the basis in the packing record: it may refer to the finished products, while net tape weight means tape material only. Carton gross weight includes the products and all included inner and outer packing. Record the basis instead of assuming these values are interchangeable.

Can one carton contain several SKUs?

Confirm the packing arrangement first. List each SKU and its quantity per carton, reconcile the total and use matching carton marks and a packing list. Mixed cartons require an agreed plan.

Can I request a custom carton mark?

Send the buyer reference, SKU description, quantity, weight basis, dimensions, carton numbering and any required artwork or barcode. Approve the mark against the final packing record before shipment.
