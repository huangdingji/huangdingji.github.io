Wholesale questions

MOQ, mixed orders and private labels.

Is there one MOQ for every wholesale PTFE tape order?

MOQ is confirmed for the selected roll specification, quantity per SKU and packing request. Neutral packs, custom spool colours, printed labels and retail boxes may have different preparation requirements. Send the intended order mix for a specific quotation.

Can I request several tape options in one inquiry?

Yes. List each product option or sample reference with its required quantity. Mixed-SKU inner packs or cartons need to be confirmed with the packing plan; a total roll quantity alone is not enough.

What should I send for private-label or OEM packing?

Send the chosen tape reference, estimated quantity per SKU, spool and pack requirements, artwork or barcode reference and destination. Product specification, artwork and finished packing are confirmed together before production.

How are sample approval and lead time handled?

Agree on the sample reference, specification and inspection basis, then confirm artwork and packing as needed. Request the production lead time and shipping scope for the final order file rather than assuming one schedule applies to every order.

Where can I compare actual tape sizes and densities?

Use the Products catalogue to filter 40 options by product type and width, then compare length, thickness and density. Return to this wholesale guide to prepare quantities, packing and order requirements.
