Privacy notice

How information is handled when you visit qzjy.store or contact FuJianTeflonTape.

Information you provide

The Contact form asks for your name and an email address or WhatsApp number so we can respond. You can also provide your company, destination country, product reference, tape specification, quantity, packing preferences, preferred reply method and a message. Fields other than your name and contact details are optional.

When you choose a product or market before contacting us, its reference and available details can be carried into the form. You can review and change those details before submitting.

How inquiry details are used

We use the information you submit to answer your inquiry, prepare a quotation and discuss related samples, specifications, OEM artwork, packing or delivery requirements. We may use your contact details to follow up on that request and any resulting business discussion.

Services involved

Form submissions are sent to Formspree for processing. Its service may also process technical information such as IP address, browser type and submission time for operation and spam prevention.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Privacy Statement.

If you choose email, WhatsApp or WeChat, your correspondence is handled through those services under their own privacy terms. Photos and attachments can be shared through those channels; this contact form does not include a file-upload field.

Website analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand page visits and interactions, including inquiry-link clicks and form submissions. Analytics can use cookies and collect usage information such as page URLs, browser and device details and approximate location.

Learn how Google uses information from websites using its services. You can manage cookies in your browser and use Google’s Analytics opt-out tool.

Where information is handled

FuJianTeflonTape is based in Quanzhou, Fujian, China. Inquiry information may be handled by us in China and by service providers in other countries, including the United States. Processing locations and provider retention practices are described in their privacy policies.

Inquiry records may remain in the form service and business correspondence while your request and any related business matters are being handled. Contact us about the retention or deletion of a particular inquiry; some records may need to be retained for ongoing business or applicable record-keeping obligations.

Your choices and privacy requests

Share only business contact and product information relevant to your request. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details or identity documents in this form.

For questions about this notice, or to request access, correction or deletion of inquiry information, email fujianteflontape@gmail.com. Include enough detail to identify the inquiry. We may need to confirm your identity before handling a request.

This notice may be updated when our website or inquiry process changes.
