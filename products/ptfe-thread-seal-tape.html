Frequently Asked Questions

What sizes does standard PTFE thread seal tape come in?

Standard PTFE thread seal tape is usually supplied in 12mm and 19mm widths, 0.075mm thickness, and lengths from 5m to 20m. Custom widths and lengths are available for OEM orders.

Can PTFE thread seal tape be used on both cold and hot water pipes?

Yes. Standard PTFE thread seal tape works on cold water, hot water, drainage and non-potable water pipe fittings. For working temperatures above 60 deg C or pressures above 10 bar, a high density PTFE tape with thicker gauge is recommended.

How many wraps of PTFE tape should I use per pipe connection?

For most water pipe fittings, 2 to 4 wraps in the direction of the thread is enough. Over-wrapping does not improve sealing and may cause fitting cracks on plastic threads.

Is PTFE tape safe for plastic and brass fittings?

Yes. PTFE tape is compatible with brass, copper, stainless steel, PVC, CPVC and most plastic fittings. It is chemically inert and does not react with standard plumbing materials.
