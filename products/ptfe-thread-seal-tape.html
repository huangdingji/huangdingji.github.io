Before you inquire

Product questions, answered.

Use the same specification and weight basis when comparing samples or supplier quotations.

Which standard 12mm × 10m options are shown here?

Options 04 and 08 are 0.075mm thick, at 0.25 and 0.35 g/cm³ density. Options 10 and 12 are 0.1mm thick, at 0.25 and 0.35 g/cm³. Their net tape weight references are 2.25g, 3.15g, 3g and 4.2g respectively.

What happens when I click “Quote this option”?

The inquiry form opens with the matching product option, dimensions, thickness and density. The message also includes the catalogue net tape weight reference. Add your quantity, destination and packing requirements before sending.

Does net tape weight include the spool and cover?

No. Net tape weight refers to the tape material only. The spool, cover and packaging belong to finished-roll or gross weight. Confirm the target, tolerance and sample reference before a bulk order.

Can the spool, label or packing be changed?

Send the required spool colour, label artwork, inner pack and carton plan. Availability, MOQ and lead time are confirmed for the chosen specification and packing request.

Is standard white tape suitable for every threaded joint?

No. Match the exact product to the fitting instructions, media and service conditions. Tape colour, thickness or a catalogue description alone does not establish application approval.
