Before you inquire

Answers before you inquire.

Use these checks to prepare a more useful product and packing inquiry.

What should a 12mm × 10m tape quotation include?

The quotation should identify width, actual roll length, thickness, density or net tape weight, spool format, inner packing and carton quantity. OEM label requirements should be listed separately.

How should buyers confirm application suitability?

Send the media, temperature, pressure, thread type and destination-market requirements. Final suitability should follow the project specification and installer guidance, not tape color or a generic product name.

Is there one wrapping count for every pipe connection?

No. The wrapping method depends on thread size, thread form, tape thickness and fitting material. Installation should follow the fitting or project instructions.

What sample information helps before a bulk order?

Send a product photo or sample, target dimensions, net tape weight or density, quantity and packing reference. The buyer should separately confirm material and application compatibility.
