Frequently Asked Questions

Is yellow PTFE tape required for gas line applications?

Yellow PTFE tape is the industry color code for gas line thread seal tape in many markets, including North America and Europe. It does not by themselves certify the tape for gas use; buyers should confirm local gas pipe standards and application requirements before use.

What thickness of yellow PTFE tape is suitable for gas fittings?

Common yellow gas PTFE tape specifications are 12mm, 18mm and 19mm widths, with thicknesses from 0.1mm to 0.2mm. Heavier gauges (0.15mm to 0.2mm) are typically used for larger gas pipe threads and higher pressure ratings.

Can I use regular white PTFE tape on gas pipes?

White PTFE tape is generally intended for water and general plumbing, not for gas. Most local codes and gas fitting standards require yellow gas-rated tape. Always follow the local installer or gas authority guidance.

Does your yellow PTFE tape meet any gas certification standard?

Our yellow gas PTFE tape is produced as a sealing component. It is the buyer's responsibility to confirm compliance with local gas pipe standards (such as EN 751-3, ASME B1.20.1 or local equivalents) before installation.
