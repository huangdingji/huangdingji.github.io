Frequently Asked Questions

Does yellow color prove that PTFE tape is approved for gas use?

No. Yellow is a product-identification choice and does not by itself certify the tape for gas use. Buyers must confirm the applicable local or project requirement before ordering and installation.

What should buyers compare when sourcing yellow PTFE tape?

Compare width, length, thickness, density or net tape weight, spool format, packing and any required product evidence. Application conditions should be supplied separately.

Can tape color replace an application review?

No. The buyer or installer must select the product according to the local requirement, media, pressure, temperature and thread system. Color alone is not a suitability decision.

How should a certification requirement be handled?

Send the exact standard, market and document requirement before quotation. This page does not claim a certification; the available evidence must be checked against the exact product being offered.
