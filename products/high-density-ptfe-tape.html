Compare on the same basis

Compare density on the same roll dimensions.

For a like-for-like 12mm × 10m comparison, standard Option 12 and high-density Option 13 are both 0.1mm thick. Their density references are 0.35 and 0.45 g/cm³, with net tape weights of 4.2g and 5.4g. Option 36 is wider and five times as long, so its 38g net tape weight is not a direct measure of density against the 12mm roll.

Use the same width, length and thickness before comparing density or net tape weight. The high-density range name does not replace a numeric specification.

Before you inquire

Product questions, answered.

Use the same specification and weight basis when comparing samples or supplier quotations.

Which high-density PTFE tape sizes are shown here?

Option 13 is 12mm × 10m × 0.1mm, with density 0.45 g/cm³ and net tape weight reference 5.4g. Option 36 is 19mm × 50m × 0.1mm, with density 0.40 g/cm³ and net tape weight reference 38g.

Is high density the same as thicker tape?

No. Both high-density options shown here are 0.1mm thick. Density is mass per unit volume; thickness is a separate dimension. Confirm both fields in the order specification.

Can I compare 5.4g and 38g as a density test?

No. The two rolls have different widths and lengths. Compare density or net tape weight on the same width, length and thickness basis; otherwise the material amounts describe different rolls.

What information reaches the inquiry form?

Choosing an option carries its product reference, dimensions, thickness and density into the form. The message includes the catalogue net tape weight reference. Quantity, destination and packing remain yours to specify.

Does the high-density range name establish a pressure or temperature rating?

No. Suitability depends on the exact finished product, fitting, media, operating conditions and installation. Confirm the required product documents and project requirements separately.

Can I request a different size, spool or label?

Send the target size, density or net tape weight, quantity and packing reference. Custom specification, sample acceptance, MOQ and lead time need confirmation before ordering.
