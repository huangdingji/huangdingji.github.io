How to Compare High-Density Tape

Compare samples only when width, length and thickness are the same. Then record density or net tape weight, winding feel and the approved packing reference.

  • Ask for the target density or net tape weight in writing.
  • Confirm the tolerance used for width, length and thickness.
  • Approve one sample before finalizing repeat-order and packing details.
  • Confirm application suitability through the buyer, installer or project specification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can one pressure rating be applied to every high-density PTFE tape?

No. Pressure suitability depends on the finished tape specification, thread type, media, temperature, joint design and installation. Ask for product-specific evidence and follow the project requirements.

How should buyers compare high-density tape with standard tape?

Compare products at the same width, length and thickness, then check density or net tape weight. The high-density label alone is not enough for a reliable quotation comparison.

Which details should be sent for product matching?

Send the target width, length, thickness, density or net tape weight, application conditions, quantity and packing method. A sample photo or physical sample is also useful.

Can you quote high-density tape for an industrial project?

We can quote a finished tape specification against the information supplied. Final application approval must follow the buyer’s project specification, installer guidance and local requirements.
