OEM PTFE Tape for Private-Label Programs

FuJianTeflonTape supplies OEM PTFE tape for overseas importers, plumbing distributors, hardware wholesalers and OEM brand customers. Send size, quantity, packaging method and target market for a factory quotation.

FuJianTeflonTape supports private-label PTFE tape programs for brand owners, importers and wholesale distributors. The tape specification is confirmed first, followed by spool, label, inner packing and carton details.

For overseas buyers, the important questions are usually practical: what width and length can be supplied, what density is suitable for the target market, how many rolls are packed in one carton, whether the label can be customized, and how stable the specification will be in repeat orders. We confirm these details before quotation so importers and distributors can compare cost, packing volume and delivery schedule more easily.

OEM PTFE tape can be supplied as a standard factory SKU or adjusted according to your existing sample. If your market already sells a certain spool color, tape color, label style or display box, you can send photos and target dimensions. We can review whether the structure is suitable for production and provide a quotation based on quantity and packaging complexity.

Artwork Confirmation Workflow

A practical OEM workflow has four checkpoints. First, confirm the tape specification and target quantity. Second, define the spool, label, barcode, inner packing and carton. Third, review the supplied artwork and agree on the proof or sample route. Fourth, confirm the approved reference before bulk production. Timing and MOQ are quoted only after the selected materials and customization scope are clear.

Frequently Asked Questions

What artwork should I send for an OEM PTFE tape inquiry?

Send the best logo or packaging file you have together with reference dimensions. We will confirm whether the format is suitable or whether a cleaner vector or higher-resolution file is needed.

What is the MOQ for custom OEM PTFE tape?

MOQ depends on the tape specification, spool and tape colors, print method, label or card material, and carton quantity. These details must be defined before an MOQ is quoted.

Which parts of the OEM presentation can be reviewed?

Spool color, tape color, label, barcode, individual wrap, display box, blister card and carton marks can be reviewed against the requested structure and artwork.

When is the OEM artwork schedule confirmed?

The schedule is confirmed after the file condition, number of packaging components, print method and proof or sample route have been reviewed.

How is the sample route decided?

The sample route depends on whether the buyer needs a standard tape sample, an existing packing reference or a custom artwork proof. Confirm the required level before placing the order.
