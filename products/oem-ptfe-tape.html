Artwork Confirmation Workflow

For OEM and private label orders, artwork confirmation follows a fixed five-step workflow to reduce sample changes and reprints. Step 1: the buyer sends the logo, label, blister card or carton artwork in a print-ready file (AI, PDF or high-resolution image). Step 2: the factory prepares a PDF layout proof with reference dimensions and Pantone or CMYK colors for the buyer's review. Step 3: the buyer marks corrections or sends written approval by email. Step 4: once the final proof is signed off, the factory keeps a digital record and uses it for printing plate or digital print setup. Step 5: a pre-production sample photo or scan is sent before bulk printing starts. This process usually takes 5 to 10 working days depending on artwork complexity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What artwork file format do I need for OEM PTFE tape?

AI, PDF, EPS and high-resolution PNG or JPG files are all accepted. Vector files (AI, PDF, EPS) are preferred for logo and label printing to ensure sharp print quality at any size.

What is the MOQ for custom OEM PTFE tape?

MOQ depends on tape specification, tape color, spool color and packaging complexity. Standard OEM programs usually start from 10,000 to 50,000 rolls. Lower MOQ may be possible for trial orders with simplified packaging.

Can you print our brand logo on the spool and individual wrap?

Yes. Custom spool color, tape color, logo printing, private label, barcode, display box and blister card can all be prepared for your OEM program.

How long does the OEM artwork confirmation process take?

From artwork receipt to signed PDF proof, the confirmation process usually takes 5 to 10 working days. Mass production starts only after written approval is received.

Can I get a sample before placing an OEM bulk order?

Yes. Plain or factory-default samples can be sent first so you can test the tape. Custom artwork samples are usually prepared together with the artwork proof and sent before bulk production.
