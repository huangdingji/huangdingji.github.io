Buyer Confirmation Before Order

Before placing a bulk order, we recommend confirming both the product specification and the packaging structure in writing. The product specification should include tape width, tape length, thickness, density, tape color and spool color. The packaging structure should include single roll packing, inner box or display box, carton quantity, carton dimensions, carton mark and any barcode or SKU label requirements. For OEM PTFE tape, artwork confirmation is especially important because the label, box and carton design must match the buyer's market. Clear confirmation before production helps reduce sample changes, quotation misunderstanding and shipment delays.

MOQ, OEM, Packing and Delivery

MOQ depends on specification, packing method and whether OEM materials are required. Standard PTFE tape with common packing usually has a lower MOQ than fully customized private label packaging. OEM orders may include logo printing, private label, custom spool color, custom tape color, barcode label, display box, blister card and export carton design.

Packing can be arranged as loose spool packing, shrink wrap, individual bag, display box, blister card or custom carton. For export orders, we suggest confirming roll quantity per carton, carton dimensions, carton mark, SKU label and destination country before production. Delivery time depends on order quantity, packaging material preparation and artwork confirmation. We can discuss sample checking before bulk production when needed.

To request a quotation, please provide width, length, thickness or density, color, quantity, packaging method, logo requirement and destination country or port. If you have a target sample, sending a clear photo helps us quote more accurately.
