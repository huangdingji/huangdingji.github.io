Wholesale selection guide

Recommended SKU direction by channel

Different customers usually need different specifications. These notes help importers, wholesalers and OEM brand customers choose a starting point before sample confirmation.

Hardware wholesale

Start with 12mm x 10m or 19mm x 15m white PTFE tape. Use simple bag or carton packing for stable repeat orders.

Gas and project supply

Use yellow, wider or thicker PTFE tape where the target market requires a clear gas line SKU. Confirm local standards before selling.

OEM brand program

Confirm tape specification first, then develop label, spool color, display box, barcode and export carton artwork.
