THE FACTORY BEHIND YOUR ORDER

A manufacturing partner.

FuJianTeflonTape is the export business name of Quanzhou Jiyu Sanitary Ware Co., Ltd., a PTFE thread seal tape manufacturer in Fujian, China.

Our in-house process covers stretching, slitting, winding and packing. We work with importers and distributors to match tape specifications, spool formats and packaging to their orders.
