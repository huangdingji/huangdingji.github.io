Customs and Import Documentation for Egypt

HS code: HS code 3920.99 (plastic plates, sheets, film, foil and strip — of other plastics). Egypt uses the Harmonised System with its own national tariff notes.

Customs duty and charges: Customs duty on plastic products (HS 3920.99) ranges from 5% to 30% of CIF value depending on the specific sub-heading and applicable trade agreement. For most Chinese-origin goods, the MFN duty is in the 10-20% range. Additional charges include 1% to 5% development surcharge, 14% VAT (since the 2016 VAT law), and possibly a 0.5% to 1% customs service fee. Total landed cost adders can reach 30-45% of CIF. Egypt has signed several trade agreements: a Free Trade Agreement with the EU, the Greater Arab Free Trade Area (GAFTA) with other Arab states, and a qualified industrial zones (QIZ) agreement with the US and Israel that can offer duty-free access to US markets for goods with sufficient Israeli input.

Standard import documents:

  • Commercial invoice (original, signed, with HS code, country of origin, and Egyptian importer details).
  • Packing list with carton-level details.
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) for ocean freight, or Air Waybill (AWB) for air freight.
  • Certificate of Origin (COO) from CCPIT — for preferential duty under GAFTA, the COO must be in the agreed format and stamped by the authorised body. For duty-free re-export to the EU, EUR.1 movement certificate is required (China-Egypt FTA is limited and does not cover HS 3920.99).
  • Egyptian customs electronic filing (Nafeza system since 2021) — the importer registers the shipment in the Nafeza / ACID system before goods arrive and receives an ACID number that must appear on all shipping documents.
  • For some product categories, a General Organisation for Export and Import Control (GOEIC) registration is required (since 2016, importers of 25 specific categories of goods must register with GOEIC; PTFE thread seal tape is generally not in this list, but verify with the importer).
  • For products that need conformity verification, an Egyptian Organisation for Standardisation (EOS) certificate or a designated third-party test report (Intertek, SGS) is required.

Ports of Entry and Air Cargo Gateways

Several commercial seaports along the Mediterranean and Red Sea coasts. Alexandria Port (the largest, 60% of Egypt's container traffic, gateway to Cairo, the Nile Delta and the Mediterranean trade). Damietta Port (30 km east of Alexandria, the second largest, modern container terminals). Port Said (the northern entry to the Suez Canal, transhipment hub for the East Mediterranean). Sokhna Port (south of Suez, on the Red Sea, gateway to Upper Egypt and the Gulf trade). Safaga, Hurghada and Nuweiba (Red Sea, smaller ports for trade with Saudi Arabia and Jordan). Air freight through Cairo International Airport (CAI, the main air gateway), Alexandria's Borg El Arab Airport and Hurghada Airport for tourist-region deliveries.

Local Market and Distribution Channels

Egypt has a population of 110 million and is the largest consumer market in the Arab world, with a fast-growing construction sector. The largest cities are Cairo (Greater Cairo region 22 million, the political and economic capital), Alexandria (5 million, the main Mediterranean port), Giza (across the Nile from Cairo), Shubra El Kheima and Port Said. The construction sector is supported by the government's New Administrative Capital (a new city 45 km east of Cairo), the New Alamein Mediterranean coastal city, and ongoing Cairo urban renewal. Demand for plumbing tape is broad-based across residential, commercial and industrial projects. Yellow gas tape is in demand from the natural gas grid expansion programme (which aims to connect most Egyptian households to natural gas by 2030, replacing LPG cylinders) and from LPG distributors in rural areas. White plumbing tape is the volume SKU across the wholesale and retail channels. Distribution is dominated by family-owned importers in Cairo and Alexandria, with growing organised retail (Carrefour Egypt, HyperOne). The Suez Canal economic zone offers duty-free manufacturing and re-export opportunities.

Seasonal Demand and Planning Notes

Demand is year-round, with a peak in Q2-Q3 (April-August) as construction projects ramp up before the cooler winter. The summer heat (June-August) increases demand for plumbing maintenance and water cooling installations. Ramadan (date shifts yearly) briefly slows customs clearance and import activity. Plan container arrival at Alexandria or Damietta 4-6 weeks before peak demand. Suez Canal transit disruptions can occasionally delay Red Sea / Sokhna arrivals; for time-sensitive orders, Mediterranean ports (Alexandria, Damietta) are more reliable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What HS code applies to PTFE tape in Egypt?

PTFE thread seal tape is classified under HS code 3920.99 (plastic plates, sheets, film, foil and strip — of other plastics). Egypt uses the HS system with national tariff notes; confirm the exact sub-heading with an Egyptian customs broker before shipping. Under the Greater Arab Free Trade Area (GAFTA), Chinese goods do not qualify for preferential duty because China is not a GAFTA member.

What is the customs duty on PTFE tape in Egypt?

Customs duty on HS 3920.99 from China is in the 10-20% range (MFN). Additional charges include 14% VAT, 1-5% development surcharge, and 0.5-1% customs service fee. Total landed cost adders can be 30-45% of CIF. There is no Egypt-China FTA covering this product, so the MFN rate applies.

Is GOEIC registration required for PTFE tape?

PTFE thread seal tape is generally not on the 25-category GOEIC registration list (which covers items such as dairy, oils, textiles, electronics and home appliances). However, the list is reviewed and updated periodically, so verify with the Egyptian importer before shipping. GOEIC registration, when required, takes about 4-6 weeks.

Which port is best for Egypt imports?

Alexandria and Damietta are the two main container ports on the Mediterranean, both well-connected to Cairo and the Nile Delta. For Upper Egypt and the Red Sea coast, Sokhna Port south of Suez is the gateway. For trans-shipment to other Mediterranean destinations, Port Said is the major hub.

What is the Nafeza / ACID system?

Nafeza is Egypt's electronic customs platform launched in 2021. All shipments to Egypt must be registered in Nafeza by the importer before arrival, and an ACID (Advance Cargo Information Declaration) number is issued. The ACID number must appear on the commercial invoice, packing list and B/L, and is used for risk profiling and customs clearance. Failure to register in Nafeza will result in the goods being held at port.
