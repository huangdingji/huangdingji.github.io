How to Prepare a Clear Import Inquiry

For faster quotation, buyers can send one message with the product type, packing method and target market. Customs and Import Documentation for UAE

Customs classification, duty, VAT and conformity requirements should be confirmed by the UAE importer or customs broker before shipment. PTFE thread seal tape is commonly reviewed as a plastic sealing or plumbing accessory, but final classification can vary by product specification, end use and customs practice.

For free zone, mainland sale, re-export or cross-border distribution, ask the local customs broker to confirm the applicable documentation, duty treatment and VAT handling before cargo dispatch.

Standard import documents:

  • Commercial invoice with product description, country of origin and the HS classification confirmed by the importer or broker.
  • Packing list (one per shipment).
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB).
  • Certificate of Origin (COO) — preferential Form A for China or general COO from CCPIT.
  • Local conformity document review if requested by the importer, customs broker or local authority.
  • For some product categories, an Emirates Authority for Standardisation and Metrology (ESMA) test report or an authorised third-party certificate (Intertek, SGS, TUV) is required.

Ports of Entry and Air Cargo Gateways

Jebel Ali Port (Dubai, a major port in the Middle East that handles a large share of UAE container traffic), Port Rashid (Dubai, secondary port for break-bulk and reefer), Khalifa Port (Abu Dhabi, the main gateway for Abu Dhabi and Al Ain), and Sharjah Port. Air freight through Dubai International Airport (DXB, one of the world's busiest cargo airports) for urgent re-orders.

Local Market and Distribution Channels

The UAE is a re-export hub: roughly 60% of imported goods are re-exported to Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Africa and South Asia. Dubai is the natural trading centre with a large Indian, Pakistani and Chinese business community familiar with Chinese suppliers. Main distribution channels: wholesale bazaars (Deira, Bur Dubai for small importers), modern hardware chains (ACE Hardware, True Value, Carrefour), and large project contractors (Arabtec, Drake & Scull for the construction boom). The construction pipeline includes Dubai Creek Harbour, Expo City legacy projects, Abu Dhabi's Masdar City, and Sharjah's industrial expansion. Demand for yellow gas tape is driven by the large LPG distribution network and the natural gas grid connecting Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the Northern Emirates. White plumbing tape is the volume SKU across all channels.
