Customs and Import Documentation for UAE

HS code: HS code 3920.99 (plastic plates, sheets, film, foil and strip — of other plastics). UAE follows the GCC Common External Tariff, harmonised with Saudi Arabia and other GCC states.

Customs duty and charges: 5% customs duty (CIF value) on plastic products under the GCC Common External Tariff. Imports into free zones (Jebel Ali Free Zone, Abu Dhabi Free Zone) are duty-free; goods released into the UAE mainland for domestic sale attract the 5% duty plus 5% VAT (since 2018). Re-exports from free zones to other GCC countries are generally duty-free under the GCC Common Market.

Standard import documents:

  • Commercial invoice (original, signed, with HS code and country of origin).
  • Packing list (one per shipment).
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB).
  • Certificate of Origin (COO) — preferential Form A for China or general COO from CCPIT.
  • SABER platform registration since 2019. SABER is the UAE's online conformity assessment platform; importers register the shipment, pay the conformity fee, and obtain a Certificate of Conformity (CoC) for the goods to clear customs.
  • For some product categories, an Emirates Authority for Standardisation and Metrology (ESMA) test report or an authorised third-party certificate (Intertek, SGS, TUV) is required.

Ports of Entry and Air Cargo Gateways

Jebel Ali Port (Dubai, the largest man-made harbour in the world and the largest port in the Middle East, handling 70% of UAE imports), Port Rashid (Dubai, secondary port for break-bulk and reefer), Khalifa Port (Abu Dhabi, the main gateway for Abu Dhabi and Al Ain), and Sharjah Port. Air freight through Dubai International Airport (DXB, one of the world's busiest cargo airports) for urgent re-orders.

Local Market and Distribution Channels

The UAE is a re-export hub: roughly 60% of imported goods are re-exported to Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Africa and South Asia. Dubai is the natural trading centre with a large Indian, Pakistani and Chinese business community familiar with Chinese suppliers. Main distribution channels: wholesale bazaars (Deira, Bur Dubai for small importers), modern hardware chains (ACE Hardware, True Value, Carrefour), and large project contractors (Arabtec, Drake & Scull for the construction boom). The construction pipeline includes Dubai Creek Harbour, Expo City legacy projects, Abu Dhabi's Masdar City, and Sharjah's industrial expansion. Demand for yellow gas tape is driven by the large LPG distribution network and the natural gas grid connecting Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the Northern Emirates. White plumbing tape is the volume SKU across all channels.

Seasonal Demand and Planning Notes

UAE imports are year-round, with a soft slowdown during Ramadan (1 month, dates shift yearly) and a moderate peak before the Dubai trade fair season (October-April, when GITEX, Big 5, Arab Health and Hardware+Tools attract international buyers). Re-export orders to Iraq, Iran and Africa spike in Q4 (October-December) as those markets prepare for their own construction season. Plan container arrival at Jebel Ali 2-3 weeks before major trade shows to allow customs clearance and warehouse positioning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What HS code applies to PTFE tape in the UAE?

PTFE thread seal tape is classified under HS code 3920.99 (plastic plates, sheets, film, foil and strip — of other plastics). The same HS code applies across the GCC, so shipments between UAE, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman and Qatar do not require reclassification.

Is SABER mandatory for PTFE tape imports?

SABER is mandatory for all goods entering the UAE mainland. The importer must register the shipment on the SABER platform, obtain a Product Conformity Certificate (PCC) from an authorised certification body (Intertek, SGS, TUV or Bureau Veritas), and pay the conformity fee. PTFE thread seal tape is generally low-risk for SABER, but the importer must complete the SABER process to clear customs.

What is the VAT on PTFE tape in the UAE?

Standard VAT is 5% on goods imported for domestic sale in the UAE mainland. Goods imported into a free zone (Jebel Ali Free Zone, etc.) and re-exported without entering the mainland are zero-rated. Goods released from a free zone into the mainland for domestic sale attract the 5% customs duty plus 5% VAT.

Can I import PTFE tape to Jebel Ali Free Zone without paying duty?

Yes. Goods imported into JAFZA and other UAE free zones are duty-free as long as they are re-exported or sold within the free zone. If the goods are later released into the UAE mainland for domestic sale, the 5% customs duty plus 5% VAT applies at that point.

What is the typical payment term for UAE importers?

For first orders, 30% T/T deposit with 70% against B/L copy is standard. Established UAE trading houses often pay L/C at sight (confirmed by a local bank such as Emirates NBD, Mashreq, ADCB) or open account 30-60 days. Cash against documents (CAD) is also common for smaller shipments.
