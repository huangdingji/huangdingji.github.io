Customs and Import Documentation for Iraq

HS code: HS code 3920.99 (plastic plates, sheets, film, foil and strip — of other plastics). Iraq follows a similar HS classification to other GCC and Middle East countries.

Customs duty and charges: Customs duty on plastic products is 5% of CIF value under the Iraqi customs tariff. Imports also attract a 0.5% reconstruction levy and a 3% customs service fee. Total landed cost adders are typically 8-10% of CIF. Goods imported via the Kurdistan Region of Iraq (KRG) follow the KRG customs tariff, which may differ on certain categories. VAT is not yet broadly applied in Iraq, but a 10% sales tax may apply on certain consumer goods.

Standard import documents:

  • Commercial invoice (original, signed, with HS code, country of origin and total value in USD).
  • Packing list with carton-level details.
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) — for ocean freight, or Air Waybill (AWB) for air freight.
  • Certificate of Origin (COO) from CCPIT (China Council for the Promotion of International Trade).
  • Iraq customs import licence held by the importer of record.
  • For some categories, a Standards and Quality Control Organisation (SQCA) test certificate, but PTFE thread seal tape is generally not in the mandatory regulated list.

Ports of Entry and Air Cargo Gateways

Two main commercial seaports: Umm Qasr Port (Basra Governorate, the main ocean gateway, 90% of Iraq's container traffic, has been modernised by a Korean consortium) and Khor Al-Zubair Port (smaller, handles general cargo). The KRG region has its own border crossings: Ibrahim Khalil (with Turkey) and Haji Omaran (for trucks), and the Fishkhabour crossing (river barge to Turkey). Air freight through Baghdad International Airport (BGW), Basra International Airport and Erbil International Airport (the main air gateway for the KRG).

Local Market and Distribution Channels

Iraq's plumbing and hardware market is fragmented and recovering from decades of conflict and under-investment. The country faces chronic housing shortages, water and sanitation infrastructure gaps, and ongoing reconstruction in cities liberated from ISIS. The largest cities by population are Baghdad (8 million), Basra (2.5 million), Mosul (2 million, post-reconstruction), Erbil (2 million, KRG capital) and Najaf / Karbala (religious tourism, drives plumbing demand for hotels and pilgrim accommodations). Demand for plumbing tape is steady, with yellow gas tape in demand from LPG distributors and the natural gas network in southern Iraq. White plumbing tape is the volume SKU across all retail and wholesale channels. The KRG region (Dohuk, Erbil, Sulaymaniyah) is a separate market with different importers, often Turkish re-export hubs. Distribution channels are mostly family-owned trading houses, with some modern hardware chains emerging in Baghdad and Erbil. Payment risk is the major concern: international letters of credit (L/C) through confirmed Iraqi banks (Bank of Baghdad, Trade Bank of Iraq, Cihan Bank for KRG) are the safest payment method, and the Trade Bank of Iraq (TBI) is the only Iraqi bank that can issue L/Cs directly to foreign suppliers without a correspondent.

Seasonal Demand and Planning Notes

Demand is year-round, with peaks before summer (May-August, when air conditioning and water cooling installations peak) and before Ramadan (when hospitality and household refurbishment increase). Re-export through Erbil to Turkey is steady in Q3-Q4 as Turkish importers prepare for the European winter season. Plan container arrival at Umm Qasr at least 4-6 weeks before major sales events to allow customs clearance, which can be slow during political uncertainty.

Frequently Asked Questions

What HS code applies to PTFE tape in Iraq?

PTFE thread seal tape is classified under HS code 3920.99 (plastic plates, sheets, film, foil and strip — of other plastics). Iraq uses the HS system but with local tariff notes; confirm with a Baghdad-based customs broker before shipping.

What is the customs duty on PTFE tape in Iraq?

Customs duty is 5% of CIF value, plus a 0.5% reconstruction levy and 3% customs service fee. Total landed cost adders are about 8-10% of CIF. There is no broad VAT, but a 10% sales tax may apply on some consumer goods.

Is L/C required for shipments to Iraq?

An L/C is strongly recommended for all but the smallest shipments, because of payment risk. The Trade Bank of Iraq (TBI) is the only Iraqi bank that can issue L/Cs directly to foreign suppliers without a correspondent; for other Iraqi banks, an L/C confirmed by an international bank (HSBC, Standard Chartered, Citi) is the safest structure.

Which port is best for Iraq imports?

Umm Qasr Port is the main ocean gateway for Iraq, handling 90% of container traffic. For the Kurdistan Region, consider transhipment through Mersin (Turkey) or direct road via the Ibrahim Khalil border crossing from Turkey. Air freight through Baghdad or Erbil is used for urgent small shipments.

Can PTFE tape be imported into the KRG separately?

Yes. The Kurdistan Regional Government operates its own customs administration with its own tariff. KRG imports often come through Turkey (Ibrahim Khalil border) and follow a separate set of duty rates and documentation. KRG importers usually hold KRG-specific import licences and can clear goods in Erbil or Dohuk without the goods physically passing through federal Iraq customs.
