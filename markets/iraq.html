Start With the Iraqi Sales Channel

A distributor supplying plumbing counters may need a compact standard roll in simple wholesale packing. A private-label hardware range may place more emphasis on spool color, bilingual artwork, barcode and shelf presentation. A project or maintenance supplier may require a clearly controlled specification and consistent repeat-order identification.

State the intended channel at the beginning of the inquiry. This helps the factory separate the product specification from the presentation and carton requirements instead of offering one generic roll for every use.

  • Plumbing and sanitary-ware wholesale distribution
  • Hardware stores and regional dealer networks
  • Building-material and maintenance supply channels
  • Valve, faucet, pipe-fitting and accessory product lines
  • Private-label or importer-owned PTFE tape ranges

Choose the Product by Specification, Not Spool Color

Spool and tape colors help identify a SKU, but they do not define performance or application suitability by themselves. Compare the complete specification and ask the buyer's technical team to confirm the intended connection, operating conditions and destination requirements.

Make Supplier Quotations Directly Comparable

A low unit price may represent a different roll length, material amount, spool or packing structure. Ask every supplier to quote the same fields and mark any substitution clearly.

Quotation fieldWhat the buyer should record
Tape specificationWidth, nominal length, thickness and density or target net tape weight if required
Finished rollTape color, spool color, spool format, winding and label
Individual packLoose roll, bag, shrink wrap, card, box or another approved format
CartonUnits per inner pack, units per carton, dimensions, net weight and gross weight
Commercial scopeQuantity by SKU, destination, requested trade term and sample requirement
Fair-comparison rule: do not compare a standard 12mm × 10m roll in simple bulk packing with a wider, longer or custom-packed roll as if they were the same product.

Prepare Arabic or Bilingual OEM Artwork Carefully

Arabic or bilingual labels can be discussed as an OEM option, but the Iraqi buyer should provide or approve the final wording. The factory should not invent translations, importer details, claims or destination-specific compliance statements.

  • Provide the brand name, approved Arabic and English copy, and logo files.
  • Identify barcode type and final number when a barcode is required.
  • Mark which product fields must appear on the roll, card, box or carton.
  • Confirm importer information, country-of-origin wording and carton marks with the buyer.
  • Approve a digital proof and physical sample before bulk printing where required.

Approve the Sample as a Complete SKU

A useful sample review covers more than tape color. Check the roll specification, winding, spool fit, label, barcode readability, individual pack and visible workmanship. If the buyer measures length, thickness, density or net tape weight, record the method and agreed tolerance with the sample.

  1. Identify the sample. Add the SKU code, revision and date.
  2. Attach the written specification. Keep all product and packing fields visible.
  3. Record requested corrections. Separate product changes from artwork changes.
  4. Approve one final version. Avoid using several partly approved samples as references.
  5. Carry the reference into repeat orders. Record later revisions instead of relying on “same as before.”

Confirm the Carton Plan Before Production

Carton quantity affects warehouse handling, product protection, marks and freight planning. It should be confirmed from the final roll and individual pack rather than copied from another SKU.

Inner packing

Record rolls per bag, shrink bundle, inner box or display box.

Export carton

Confirm quantity, dimensions, net weight and gross weight.

Carton marks

Approve SKU, quantity, buyer mark, destination and handling text.

Mixed orders

Separate every SKU and carton quantity on the packing specification.

If several lengths, spool colors or labels share one order, use a separate line for each SKU. The carton mark and packing list should make the differences easy to identify on arrival.

Confirm Iraq Import Requirements With the Importer

Classification, customs duties, documentation, labeling and conformity requirements can change and may depend on the exact product, importer, destination and shipment arrangement. The Iraqi importer or appointed customs broker should confirm the current requirements before production and shipment.

The buyer should also confirm the destination, consignee details, requested trade term and any document or carton-mark requirement before the quotation is finalized. The supplier can then prepare available commercial and packing information against the approved order rather than relying on generic market assumptions.

No automatic compliance assumption: spool color, tape color or a product name does not prove that a SKU meets a particular local rule or application requirement.

Send a Quotation-Ready Iraq Inquiry

A complete first message reduces repeated questions and makes supplier comparisons more useful.

Inquiry structure: buyer channel; width × nominal length × thickness; density or target net tape weight if required; tape and spool colors; label language; individual pack; carton requirements; quantity by SKU; destination; sample and inspection requirements.

If some fields are unknown, send a photo or existing sample and identify the decisions that still need confirmation. For broader factory-direct purchasing guidance, see the PTFE tape wholesale sourcing page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information is needed for a PTFE tape quotation for Iraq?

Send the tape width, length, thickness, density or net tape weight if required, tape and spool colors, packing, label, quantity and destination. Unknown fields can be marked for confirmation.

Can PTFE tape for Iraq use an Arabic private label?

Arabic or bilingual labels can be reviewed as an OEM option when the buyer provides or approves the final wording, artwork, barcode and required product or importer information.

Should an importer approve a sample before a bulk order?

A physical sample helps align the tape specification, spool, winding, label and packing. The approved sample and written specification should be kept together for production and repeat orders.

Can different PTFE tape SKUs be packed in one order?

Different SKUs can be discussed, but each specification, label, inner pack, carton quantity and carton mark should be identified separately before quotation and production.

Who confirms Iraq import documents and local requirements?

The Iraqi importer or appointed customs broker should confirm current classification, import documents, duties, labeling and any destination-specific conformity requirement before production and shipment.
